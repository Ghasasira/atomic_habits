Privacy Policy

Atomic Habits (Android) · Effective 19 July 2026

In short: Atomic Habits collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your device, and the app never connects to the internet.

Who we are

Atomic Habits is an offline habit and goal tracker developed by Gasasira. For any questions about this policy, contact gasasiraj013@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party services. It does not request internet access, so nothing you do in the app can be transmitted anywhere.

Where your data lives

The habits, goals, logs, and settings you create are stored in a private SQLite database inside the app's own storage on your device. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not visible to other apps;
  • is not accessible to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions the app uses

These permissions exist solely to make habit reminders ring on time:

  • Notifications — to show your habit reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — so a reminder fires at the exact minute you chose, even when the device is idle. If declined, reminders still work but may be slightly delayed.
  • Run at startup — to re-arm your reminders after the phone reboots.
  • Full-screen notifications & vibration — so a reminder can behave like an alarm.

None of these permissions read, collect, or transmit any data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it also collects no data from children.

Deleting your data

You are in full control. Deleting a habit or goal removes it and its history from the database. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently erases all app data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled (for example, adding an optional backup feature), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
